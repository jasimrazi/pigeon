## Notes on Classification

1. **All items were classified** into BOOTSTRAP, SECRET, SETTING, or INVARIANT. No unclassifiable configuration items were identified.
2. **Bootstrap Secrets**: `SETTINGS_ENCRYPTION_KEY` (or `GOOGLE_TOKEN_ENCRYPTION_KEY`), `CF_ACCESS_AUD`, `CF_ACCESS_TEAM_DOMAIN`, `CF_ACCESS_ALLOWED_EMAIL`, and `OWNER_GMAIL` remain in Cloudflare Worker secrets because they are required before D1 can be read or trusted.
3. **Migrated Secrets**: Google OAuth credentials (`GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`), `GEMINI_API_KEY`, and `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` are moved out of environment variables into the encrypted `app_settings` D1 table, managed directly from the UI.
